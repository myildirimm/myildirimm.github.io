Welcome.

I develop intelligent decision-making and control methods for autonomous systems.

I am a Lecturer in the Department of Mechanical Engineering at İstanbul University–Cerrahpaşa. My research combines reinforcement learning, model-based control, and interpretable AI to make autonomous vehicles and robots safer, more capable, and easier to validate.

I completed my PhD at the University of Surrey in 2025, where I worked on intelligent decision-making for autonomous driving. My background spans academic research and automotive engineering, including roles with Innovate UK projects, Sellafield Game Changer, Ford Otosan, and AVL.

Research

Learning and planning for systems that act under uncertainty.

My work sits at the intersection of control engineering, artificial intelligence, robotics, and intelligent mobility.

01

Autonomous decision-making

POMDPs, Monte Carlo tree search, trajectory prediction, and interactive planning for complex traffic.

02

Reinforcement & imitation learning

Safe policy learning, behavioural cloning, human-like driving, and multi-agent coordination.

03

Interpretable autonomy

Neuro-symbolic learning and language-model reasoning for explainable, knowledge-informed policies.

04

Control & optimisation

Model predictive control, feedback control, and hybrid model-based / learning-based architectures.

05

Simulation & validation

Closed-loop testing, scenario evaluation, digital twins, and reproducible benchmarking.

06

Autonomous flight

Minimal-sensor reinforcement learning and flight-dynamics simulation for autonomous thermal soaring.

Currently

Working on safe and interpretable driving policies, minimal-sensor autonomous soaring, and multi-agent learning for coordinated vehicle and robot control.
